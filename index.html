About the lodge

Everything you need, and nothing you don't.

Chilambula View Lodge is a family-run bed and breakfast in Area 6, off Maula Prison Road along Paul Kagame Road. We built it for people who want a comfortable bed at a fair price, a proper meal without leaving the gate, and somewhere to hold a meeting in the morning — all in the same place, so the day runs without a single taxi ride.

Close to the centre

Minutes from Lilongwe’s Old Town and a short drive to City Centre offices and Kamuzu Central Hospital.

Fair, honest rates

Budget pricing with no hidden extras. Breakfast, Wi-Fi and parking are part of the room, not an add-on.

Looked after in person

The proprietor is on site. If something isn’t right, you speak to the person who can fix it.
